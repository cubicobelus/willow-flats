# Willow Flats

A city-building game in your browser. Lay out streets and zones, keep power, water and waste
running, and grow a settlement into a city while balancing the treasury and your residents'
happiness. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file, no build step.

## Play

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://cubicobelus.github.io/willow-flats/city_planner.html)**: nothing to download or install.
Use the in-game **Tutorial** button to get started.

To run it offline, download `city_planner.html` and open it. (Fonts load from Google Fonts, so
it looks best with an internet connection.)

## Features

- Residential, commercial and industrial zones driven by live demand
- Power, water and waste networks, plus fire, police, health, school and transit coverage
- Map overlays for land value, traffic, crime and pollution
- Difficulty levels and seeded maps, undo, and save and load in your browser
- Zoom and pan
